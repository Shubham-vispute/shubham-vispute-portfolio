RTOS & communication protocols

A quick revision guide to task coordination and common embedded interfaces.

RTOS fundamentals

  • Task/thread: an independently schedulable execution context.
  • Priority scheduling: higher-priority ready tasks commonly preempt lower-priority tasks in a preemptive RTOS.
  • Mutex: protects shared resources and may support priority inheritance.
  • Semaphore: signals events or counts available resources; binary and counting forms are common.
  • ISR: interrupt service routines should be short; use RTOS-approved ISR-safe APIs.
  • Deadlock: can result from circular resource dependencies; consistent lock ordering and short critical sections help reduce risk.

Debugging approach

Capture the traffic where possible, verify baud rate or bus timing, check addressing and termination, confirm framing and checksums, add bounded timeouts, and log errors without blocking time-critical paths.

Protocol behaviour depends on the standard, physical layer, device implementation and system configuration. Use the device datasheet and protocol specification as the source of truth.
